Why horaria

Built for planners.

  • Real-time collaboration

    Multiple planners work on the same timetable — changes are immediately visible to everyone.

  • Publish & freeze

    Planning periods are published at a fixed state and are immutable afterwards — corrections run in a new period.

  • Open formats

    GTFS import and export are part of the core. Data sovereignty stays with you.

  • For teams & agencies

    Multiple companies in one installation — each organisation with its own data.

Technology & operations

Cloud-native, vendor-independent, cost-effective.

horaria runs on modern cloud infrastructure — scaling with your operation and without idle costs. And it runs where you want it: hosted by us or in your own infrastructure.

  • Scale-to-Zero

    Without usage, the platform scales to zero — you only pay for what you actually use, not for idle time.

  • Cloud-agnostic

    AWS, Azure, Google Cloud or another provider: horaria stays replaceable — no vendor lock-in.

  • Your own Kubernetes cluster

    For maximum data sovereignty, horaria can be integrated into your own Kubernetes infrastructure — on-premises or with your cloud provider.

  • A cost-effective alternative

    Modern architecture without enterprise overhead: the predictable, affordable alternative to established planning systems.
